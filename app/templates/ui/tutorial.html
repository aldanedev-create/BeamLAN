Quick tutorial

How to use LanDrop

Move files directly between nearby devices without an account or cloud upload.

01

Connect devices

Put devices on the same Wi-Fi network or connect them to the same phone hotspot. Open Connect phone to scan the QR code.

02

Phone to PC

Open the LanDrop address on your phone, choose a file, and press send. The Windows app opens an approval page before saving the file.

03

PC to PC

Open Send, select a discovered device, choose a file, and wait for the receiver to approve it.

04

PC to phone

From Send, choose “Create phone share.” Scan the generated QR code on the phone and download the temporary file page.

When something does not connect

  • Confirm every device is on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot.
  • Allow LanDrop through Windows Firewall on Private networks.
  • Use the computer's IPv4 address from ipconfig if QR discovery is unavailable.
  • Do not expose the local server to the public internet. Temporary links are intended for trusted local networks.
